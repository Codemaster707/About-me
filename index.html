Curious about what’s beyond the boundary.

I grew up in a Marathi family of doctors. My father is a pediatrician and my mother is an Ayurvedic doctor. Jalna is a city where most people follow practical, predictable paths. I always wanted to know what existed outside them.

During the COVID-19 lockdown I was eight and in third grade. Online classes put my father’s old Windows 7 Dell laptop in my hands. I found the internet, and I haven’t stopped exploring since.

How I got here

Not a straight line. Idea, experiment, failure, lesson, new idea.

  • 2012

    Born in Jalna

    Met Dhairya in Junior KG. Years later we would code together.

  • 2020

    The old laptop

    Lockdown, online classes, and a Windows 7 Dell. I even downloaded a virus while hunting for GTA Vice City. It made me more curious, not less.

  • Early experiments

    Trying every way to make money online

    Print-on-demand, dropshipping, digital products, YouTube, Canva design, internet marketing, stock-market basics, Fiverr, Shopify and website building. Most failed, and every one taught me something.

  • 7th grade

    Python changed the question

    Dhairya and I started learning Python. I stopped asking “how do I use this?” and started asking “how do I build this?”

  • First real build

    A website for my family’s hospital

    My parents had a quote of about ₹10,000 for a hospital website. I decided to build it myself. Minakshi Bal Rugnalaya went live on its own domain.

  • 21 May 2026

    UrjaRise.com is born

    I bought the domain and launched. Frontend, backend, database, login, hosting and Growth Cards were all built by me.

  • October 2026

    A new six-member team

    After my first Discord team fell apart, I chose to try again, with clearer roles this time.

Things I’ve built

Some live, some failed. All of them are why UrjaRise exists.

Live at urjarise.com

UrjaRise

A mix of social network and productivity platform. Track progress, share achievements, build accountability, earn recognition and compete in a positive way. The question behind it: what if social media encouraged progress instead of distraction?

🃏 Collectible Growth Cards
💎 Card rarities and inventory
⚡ Urja Points
🎓 Educational version in progress

Lesson: A website isn’t a community, and a domain isn’t an audience. Getting people to care is the other half of building.

Live

Minakshi Bal Rugnalaya

The official website for my family’s hospital, built from scratch instead of paying for it.

Lesson: Real people can use what you make.

Struggled

Softwave

My first attempt at a real startup, a platform for people starting out in web services. It was also my first serious contact with Firebase and backend code.

Lesson: Execution and technical foundations matter more than the idea.

Too big

SuperNet

A vision for a parallel digital world where people connect, learn, work and create. It was far beyond my resources, but it taught me to think in ecosystems.

Lesson: Think big, then shrink it until it ships.

First stranger user

DailyHub

An all-in-one productivity platform for goals, tools and self-improvement. A stranger actually used it, a tiny milestone that felt huge.

Lesson: A real user is different from an idea.

Led to UrjaRise

SkillOrigo

A way to discover skill-learning opportunities near you. It raised a question I couldn’t shake: what makes someone come back tomorrow?

Lesson: Build for return visits: community, identity, progress.
